# Multi-Company Support Reference
Document ID: KB-011
Document Type: Customer Support
Last Updated: 2026-02-15

NexaTech:
- Standard support: Monday–Friday, 09:00–18:00 IST.
- Premium Support: 24/7 incident response.

Orbit Analytics:
- Standard support: Monday–Friday, 08:00–17:00 IST.
- Priority customers receive extended incident support; exact hours are not specified.

Zenith Robotics:
- Standard support: Monday–Friday, 09:00–18:00 IST.
- Enterprise customers: 24/7 critical-incident escalation.

ClearPath Health Systems:
- Standard support: Monday–Friday, 09:00–18:00 IST.
- Enterprise customers may purchase 24/7 critical-incident response.

Evergreen Energy Systems:
- Standard support: Monday–Friday, 09:00–18:00 IST.
- Premium customers: 24/7 critical-incident escalation.

Helix FinTech:
- Standard support: Monday–Friday, 09:00–18:00 IST.
- Enterprise customers: 24/7 critical-incident response.

Important: 24/7 critical-incident response does not mean that standard support is available 24/7.

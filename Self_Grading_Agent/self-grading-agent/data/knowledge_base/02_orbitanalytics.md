# Orbit Analytics — Company Profile
Document ID: KB-002

Last Updated: 2026-02-15

Orbit Analytics is a fictional data analytics company founded in 2018 and headquartered in Hyderabad, India. It provides business intelligence, forecasting, and operational analytics software.

Leadership:
- Kavya Menon — Chief Executive Officer, since March 2022.
- Arjun Shah — Chief Technology Officer, since September 2021.
- Priya Nair — Chief Product Officer, since June 2024.

Primary products:
- OrbitBI — business intelligence and dashboarding platform.
- ForecastPro — demand forecasting platform.
- InsightFlow — operational analytics product.

Markets: India, UAE, and Singapore.

OrbitBI launched in April 2022. ForecastPro launched in August 2023. InsightFlow launched in January 2025.

OrbitBI Professional is priced at $29 per user per month with a 10-seat minimum. ForecastPro is priced at $99 per forecasting workspace per month. InsightFlow pricing is quote-based.

Orbit Analytics provides hosted services using TLS 1.3 in transit and AES-256 at rest. Its standard hosted deployment region is Mumbai, India, with a 99.95% monthly availability target.

Standard customer support operates Monday–Friday, 08:00–17:00 IST. Priority customers receive extended incident support.

Evergreen Energy Systems is a fictional clean-energy software company founded in 2016 and headquartered in Ahmedabad, India. It provides software for solar asset monitoring, energy forecasting, and maintenance operations.

Leadership:
- Dev Patel — Chief Executive Officer, since July 2019.
- Neha Desai — Chief Technology Officer, since April 2021.
- Manoj Rao — Chief Operating Officer, since January 2022.

Primary products:
- SolarWatch — solar asset monitoring.
- GridForecast — renewable-energy forecasting.
- FieldOps — maintenance workflow management.

Markets: India, Australia, and UAE.

SolarWatch launched in June 2020. GridForecast launched in September 2022. FieldOps launched in February 2024.

SolarWatch is priced at $59 per monitored site per month. GridForecast is priced at $129 per forecasting workspace per month. FieldOps is quote-based.

Hosted services use TLS 1.3 in transit and AES-256 at rest. The standard hosted deployment region is Mumbai, India, with a 99.9% monthly availability target.

Standard support is Monday–Friday, 09:00–18:00 IST. Premium customers receive 24/7 critical-incident escalation.

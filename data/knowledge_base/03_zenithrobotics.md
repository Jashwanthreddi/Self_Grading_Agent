# Zenith Robotics — Company Profile
Document ID: KB-003
Document Type: Company Profile
Last Updated: 2026-02-15

Zenith Robotics is a fictional industrial automation company founded in 2017 and headquartered in Pune, India. It develops robotic inspection, warehouse automation, and fleet-management software.

Leadership:
- Sameer Kulkarni — Chief Executive Officer, since February 2020.
- Elena Thomas — Chief Technology Officer, since November 2022.
- Vikram Joshi — Chief Operating Officer, since May 2023.

Primary products:
- VisionInspect — AI-assisted visual inspection.
- RoboFleet — robotic fleet management.
- WarehousePilot — warehouse automation orchestration.

Markets: India, Germany, and Japan.

VisionInspect launched in February 2021. RoboFleet launched in October 2022. WarehousePilot launched in July 2024.

VisionInspect is priced at $149 per inspection station per month. RoboFleet is priced at $299 per robot per month. WarehousePilot uses annual enterprise contracts with quote-based pricing.

Hosted management services use TLS 1.3 for network encryption. Customer configuration data is encrypted at rest using AES-256. The standard hosted control plane is located in Frankfurt, Germany.

Standard support is Monday–Friday, 09:00–18:00 IST. Enterprise customers receive 24/7 critical-incident escalation.

Important scope: These facts are for the fictional benchmark and should not be treated as real-world company information.

# Helix FinTech — Company Profile
Document ID: KB-006
Document Type: Company Profile
Last Updated: 2026-02-15

Helix FinTech is a fictional financial technology company founded in 2021 and headquartered in Mumbai, India. It develops software for payment operations, reconciliation, and financial risk monitoring.

Leadership:
- Aditya Rao — Chief Executive Officer, since November 2021.
- Sara Fernandes — Chief Technology Officer, since February 2022.
- Ishan Gupta — Chief Risk Officer, since September 2023.

Primary products:
- PayOps — payment operations management.
- ReconX — financial reconciliation platform.
- RiskLens — transaction risk monitoring.

Markets: India, Singapore, and UAE.

PayOps launched in January 2023. ReconX launched in June 2023. RiskLens launched in October 2024.

PayOps is priced at $0.015 per processed transaction plus a platform fee. ReconX is priced at $79 per reconciliation workspace per month. RiskLens is quote-based.

Hosted services use TLS 1.3 in transit and AES-256 at rest. The standard hosted deployment region is Singapore, with a 99.95% monthly availability target.

Standard support operates Monday–Friday, 09:00–18:00 IST. Enterprise customers receive 24/7 critical-incident response.

# NexaTech Solutions — Company Profile

Last Updated: 2026-02-15

NexaTech Solutions is a fictional enterprise technology company founded in 2019 and headquartered in Bengaluru, India. It develops software for enterprise search, workflow automation, and data governance.

Leadership:
- Rohan Mehta — Chief Executive Officer, since January 2021.
- Anika Rao — Chief Technology Officer, since July 2023.
- Meera Iyer — Chief Operating Officer, since April 2024.

Primary products:
- NovaSearch — enterprise knowledge search and question answering.
- FlowPilot — workflow automation platform.
- DataMesh Hub — data catalog and governance platform.

Markets: India and Singapore.

NovaSearch launched in March 2025. FlowPilot launched in September 2024. DataMesh Hub launched in November 2023.

NovaSearch Enterprise is listed at $49 per user per month with a 25-seat minimum. FlowPilot Business is listed at $79 per workspace per month and includes up to 20 active workflows. DataMesh Hub uses quote-based pricing.

NexaTech's hosted production services use TLS 1.3 for data in transit and AES-256 for data at rest. Standard hosted deployments use infrastructure in Singapore and have a 99.9% monthly availability target.

Customer support is available Monday–Friday, 09:00–18:00 IST for standard support. Premium Support provides 24/7 incident response.

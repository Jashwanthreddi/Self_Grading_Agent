# Multi-Company Product Catalog
Document ID: KB-007
Document Type: Product Catalog
Last Updated: 2026-02-15

This document consolidates product names and primary functions across the benchmark companies.

NexaTech Solutions:
- NovaSearch — enterprise knowledge search and question answering.
- FlowPilot — workflow automation.
- DataMesh Hub — data catalog and governance.

Orbit Analytics:
- OrbitBI — business intelligence and dashboards.
- ForecastPro — demand forecasting.
- InsightFlow — operational analytics.

Zenith Robotics:
- VisionInspect — AI-assisted visual inspection.
- RoboFleet — robotic fleet management.
- WarehousePilot — warehouse automation orchestration.

ClearPath Health Systems:
- CareFlow — clinical workflow coordination.
- ClinicDesk — outpatient administration.
- MedTrack — medication workflow management.

Evergreen Energy Systems:
- SolarWatch — solar asset monitoring.
- GridForecast — renewable-energy forecasting.
- FieldOps — maintenance workflow management.

Helix FinTech:
- PayOps — payment operations management.
- ReconX — financial reconciliation.
- RiskLens — transaction risk monitoring.

Important: Product names that look similar must not be treated as interchangeable. A product belongs only to the company listed above.

# ClearPath Health Systems — Company Profile
Document ID: KB-004
Document Type: Company Profile
Last Updated: 2026-02-15

ClearPath Health Systems is a fictional healthcare software company founded in 2020 and headquartered in Chennai, India. It develops administrative and clinical workflow software for hospitals and clinics.

Leadership:
- Nikhil Varma — Chief Executive Officer, since August 2021.
- Dr. Asha Kapoor — Chief Medical Technology Officer, since January 2023.
- Rahul Bose — Chief Operating Officer, since October 2024.

Primary products:
- CareFlow — clinical workflow coordination.
- ClinicDesk — outpatient administration.
- MedTrack — medication workflow management.

Markets: India and Malaysia.

CareFlow launched in May 2022. ClinicDesk launched in March 2023. MedTrack launched in December 2024.

CareFlow Enterprise is priced at $89 per clinical user per month. ClinicDesk is priced at $39 per staff user per month. MedTrack uses quote-based pricing.

Hosted services use TLS 1.3 in transit and AES-256 at rest. The standard hosted deployment region is Singapore. The standard availability target is 99.9% per month.

Standard support operates Monday–Friday, 09:00–18:00 IST. Enterprise customers can purchase 24/7 critical-incident response.

Important scope: These facts are for the fictional benchmark and should not be treated as real-world company information.
